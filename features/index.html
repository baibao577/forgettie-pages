Every feature of Forgettie

The complete reference — each one in a line or two.

Yearly total across every subscription
Annualized cost view. Monthly, yearly, quarterly and one-off billings all normalized into a single per-year number, so the $9.99 quietly becomes $120 and the decision gets easier.
Cash-flow forecast for the coming month
See what's due when, so you can plan your cash flow, move money around, or cancel before the next charge arrives — not after it's already hit.
Price change tracking with effective dates
Your streaming sub went from $9.99 to $14.99 in July? Log the change and the date. Past billings keep the old amount, new billings use the new one, and your year-to-date total stays accurate instead of silently jumping.
Pause and resume without losing the math
Cancel in February, resume in April, cancel again in June, resume in August — every gap is remembered. Only the months you actually paid count toward your total. Works for any number of stop-resume cycles.
Quiet reminders before every renewal
Surface what's due soon right when you open the app. No email spam, no push nagging until you actually ask for it. Configurable lead time so reminders arrive when you want them.
Calendar export (.ics) to Google, Apple, Outlook
One-tap .ics export — the reminder lives in the calendar you already look at. No extra notification system to manage, no separate inbox.
Tag and group by category
Tag each item (Streaming, Insurance, Domains…) and group by tag to see category totals at a glance. Filter is any, not all, so broad searches stay fast.
Works offline as a PWA
Install to your home screen on iOS, Android, or desktop. Opens in its own window and keeps working without a connection. Your list never depends on a server being up.
Private by design — no account, no bank login
No account. No sign-up. No bank integration. Your list lives on your device in an IndexedDB store. Nothing syncs to a server unless you choose to back it up yourself.
Dark mode, from day one
Follows your system preference, with a hand-tuned dark theme that respects OLED displays. Manual toggle available too, if you prefer to override.
Free, forever
Zero cost. One-time install. No subscription for the subscription tracker — that would defeat the point.
Savings tracker on cancelled items
When you cancel a subscription, Forgettie keeps counting what you would have spent and shows it as money back in your pocket. Every cancel makes the number tangible.
