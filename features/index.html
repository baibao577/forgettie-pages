What Forgettie does

Everything Forgettie does — in one page.

Forgettie is an offline subscription tracker for people who want to see the real cost of their recurring bills — without handing over bank credentials or signing up for yet another account. Here's exactly what it does.

See the yearly total of every subscription

Annualized cost view — monthly, yearly, one-off all normalized into what it really costs you per year. The $9.99 quietly becomes $120 and the decision gets easier.

$15.99/mo → $191.88/yr

Forecast next month's bills before they land

See what's due when, so you can plan your cash flow, move money around, or cancel before the next charge arrives — not after.

Quiet reminders before every renewal

Surface what's due soon right when you open the app. No email spam, no push nagging until you actually ask for it.

Export any due date to your calendar

One-tap .ics export — the reminder lives in Google, Apple, or Outlook Calendar where you already look. No extra notification system to manage.

Tag and group by category

Tag each item (Streaming, Insurance, Domains…) and group by tag to see category totals. Filter is any, not all, so broad searches stay fast.

Works offline as a PWA

Install to your home screen on iOS, Android, or desktop. Opens in its own window and works without a connection. Your list never depends on a server.

Private by design

No account. No sign-up. No bank login. Your list lives on your device. Nothing syncs to a server unless you choose to back it up yourself.

Dark mode, from day one

Follows your system, with a hand-tuned dark theme that respects OLED. Manual toggle too, if you prefer.

Free, forever

Zero cost. One-time install. No subscription for the subscription tracker — that would defeat the point.
